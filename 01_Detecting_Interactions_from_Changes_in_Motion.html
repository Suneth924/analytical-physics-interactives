What to notice

  • Equal-length, parallel velocity arrows represent unchanged velocity.
  • Equal spacing between sampled positions represents constant speed.
  • No net interaction does not mean no interactions at all; separate interactions may balance.
Newton's First Law An object remains at rest or continues with constant velocity unless a net interaction changes its velocity.

Velocity includes both speed and direction. Changing either one means the velocity has changed.
